## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-precision computational agronomy and ecological physiology. Designed for agronomists, plant biologists, environmental researchers, and precision farm operators, it projects an uncompromising standard of scientific rigor combined with biological intuition.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Technical Clean** with **Data-Dense Environmental Minimalism**:
- **Pristine Precision**: Crisp scientific containers, calibrated scales, metric-dense arrangements, and exact numeric readouts.
- **Ecological Vibrancy**: Lush emerald and deep chlorophyll greens root the interface in organic life, counterbalanced by laboratory-grade slate and mineral neutrals.
- **Dynamic Stress Signals**: Sharp amber and thermal coral accents instantly expose abiotic stress triggers (heat shock, water deficit, osmotic salinity pressure).
- **Tactile Simulation Surfaces**: Micro-sliders, parametric toggles, and live physiological graphs evoke precision laboratory instrumentation.

## Colors

The palette references living photosynthetic tissues, climatic stress thresholds, and laboratory instrument panels:

- **Primary (`#1B663E`) - Chlorophyll Vitality**: Anchors key physiological status indicators, primary actions, and optimal biometric states. Reflects vegetative health and vigorous canopy growth.
- **Secondary (`#D9532F`) - Acute Thermal & Saline Stress**: Signals severe abiotic disruptions such as thermal leaf scorching, hyper-osmotic shock, and stomatal closure collapse.
- **Tertiary (`#D9822B`) - Threshold Warning / Transpiration Deficit**: Highlights creeping physiological stress, sub-optimal soil moisture, and moderate vapor pressure deficits.
- **Neutral (`#4A5568`) - Mineral Slate**: Supports data grids, scientific axes, measurement annotations, and boundary definitions without competing with biological visualization channels.

### Functional Palette Nuances
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAFC` provides an ultra-clean, glare-free optical ground.
- **Surface Elevation**: `#FFFFFF` cards framed by calibrated hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Telemetry Readout Backgrounds**: Subdued slate-tinted panels (`#EDF2F7` / `#F1F5F9`) preserve high signal-to-noise ratio in multi-chart analytical views.

## Typography

The typographic structure balances neutral structural readability with monospaced numerical clarity:

- **Primary Engine (Inter)**: Handles analytical dashboards, system navigation, stage descriptions, and explanatory physiology briefs. High x-height and precise geometric shapes ensure effortless legibility under dense multi-column layouts.
- **Instrumentation & Metric Engine (JetBrains Mono)**: Exclusively drives numerical data tables, sensor streams, environmental vector parameters (°C, dS/m, kPa, % VWC), coordinates, and time-step sequences. Tabular alignment ensures instant vertical visual scanning across dynamic simulation steps.
- **Hierarchy Rules**:
  - `metric-display` is paired with `label-sm` in uppercase tracking for rapid parameter monitoring.
  - Section dividers, stage titles, and model state labels maintain compact tracking to maximize visual real estate for interactive visualizers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a strictly disciplined 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for responsive real-time dashboards:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**:
  - 12-column structure with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins.
  - Asymmetric analytical split: 3 columns for environmental stress manipulation inputs (continuous range sliders, presets, timeline scrubbers), 6 columns for the central 2D/3D canopy and cellular visualizer, and 3 columns for real-time telemetry (osmotic balance, leaf water potential, biomass projection).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**:
  - 8-column structure with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins.
  - The biological visualizer occupies the top 8 columns; control sliders and sensor readouts stack in split 4-column blocks below.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**:
  - 4-column single-column flow with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` safe margins.
  - Visualizer anchors in a persistent sticky viewport; environmental parameter controls collapse into segmented drawer tabs.
- **Component Spacing**:
  - Card internal padding standardizes on `space-md` (`1rem`) for dense metric clusters and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for simulation canvas wrappers.
  - Compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments govern close parameter associations (e.g., metric label to monospaced value).

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve laboratory-grade visual cleanliness and prevent optical clutter in data-dense displays, visual layering relies on **Tonal Stratification and Low-Contrast Technical Hairlines** rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0)**: Page background (`#F8FAFC`). Flat, non-reflective canvas.
- **Surface Layer (Elevation 1)**: Operational cards, visualizer frame, and control sidebars (`#FFFFFF`). Defined by a razor-sharp 1px boundary (`#E2E8F0`) paired with an ultra-subtle ambient tint: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive & Nested Layer (Elevation 2)**: Sliders, active simulation monitors, and tab groups (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`). Employs soft inner hair-borders and an ambient lift: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Overlay & Dynamic Inspector (Elevation 3)**: Tooltips, cellular inspection modals, context-sensitive gene expression tooltips, and floating playback bars (`#FFFFFF` with `#0F172A` text): `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Critical Alert Surface**: Elevated stress breach panels employ a thin 1px border tinted in accent coral (`#D9532F`) with a 4% alpha surface wash, creating immediate focal salience without elevation jumps.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft Scientific Corner Matrix (Level 1)**, ensuring crisp structural discipline suitable for precision laboratory software:

- **Core Elements (`rounded-md`, 0.25rem / 4px)**: Input steppers, slider thumbs, metric badge chips, radio markers, checkbox frames, and chart data tooltips.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Simulation viewport frames, analytical panels, parameter configuration cards, and dynamic legend sheets.
- **Global Modals & Inspector Dialogs (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px)**: Deep dive crop physiology modal inspector and timeline trajectory drawers.
- **Visualizer Anchors**: Internal SVG/Canvas elements (chloroplast representation, xylem transport channels, root mesh lines) adopt precise architectural geometries with minimal rounding to reflect anatomical fidelity.

## Components

### Parameter Sliders & Dial Inputs
- **Track**: 4px height, neutral stone (`#E2E8F0`), with dynamic stress gradient fills (green to amber to coral) reflecting critical biological thresholds.
- **Thumb**: 18px diameter, solid `#FFFFFF` with a 2px `#1B663E` border and active focus ring. When manipulating extreme stress zones, thumb border transitions smoothly to `#D9532F`.
- **Value Readout**: Adjacent right-aligned `JetBrains Mono` label displaying instantaneous unit values (e.g., `38.5 °C`, `12.4 dS/m`).

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Simulation Trigger (Run / Advance Step)**: Filled `#1B663E`, white text, 0.25rem corner radius, font weight 600. Hover state darkens to `#144D2F`.
- **Secondary / Baseline Reset**: Clean white surface with 1px `#CBD5E1` border, text `#4A5568`, hover state `#F1F5F9`.
- **Stress Injection Toggles**: Segmented pills with monospaced toggles (`NORM`, `MILD`, `CRIT`). Selected states use high-contrast tinting matched to the respective stress factor.

### Metric Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- Flat `#FFFFFF` container with 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Top row: Uppercase metric label in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-sm`), `#64748B`.
- Center row: Large dynamic readout (`metric-display`) with inline delta indicators (e.g., `↓ 14% Leaf Transpiration`).
- Bottom row: Inline sparkline or threshold bar highlighting proximity to wilting point.

### Biological Visualizer Canvas Wrapper
- Dark or light calibrated backdrop with low-opacity grid lines (5% slate).
- Integrated HUD overlay featuring floating step controllers (Step Forward, Play/Pause, Rewind, Delta Time multiplier).
- Anatomical layers toggle group (Root System, Stem Xylem, Stomatal Aperture, Canopy LAI).

### Chips & Biological Status Badges
- Height: 24px, padding: 0 8px, border radius: 4px.
- Typography: `label-sm` (`JetBrains Mono`).
- State styles:
  - *Optimal*: Background `#EAF5EE`, border `#C2E2CC`, text `#1B663E`.
  - *Transpiration Strain*: Background `#FEF6EE`, border `#FCD8B5`, text `#D9822B`.
  - *Osmotic/Thermal Crisis*: Background `#FDF2F0`, border `#FAC5BD`, text `#D9532F`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radios
- Square checkbox (`16px x 16px`), 3px border radius. Unchecked `#CBD5E1` border, checked `#1B663E` fill with sharp white check vector.
- Segmented time-scale selectors (`1h`, `6h`, `24h`, `7d`, `Full Season`) styled in unified pill frames with smooth sliding active background.
