## Brand & Style

The design system embodies agricultural stewardship, technological precision, and environmental resilience tailored for the agrarian realities of the Mekong Delta. Designed for a high-stakes climate simulation and on-chain agricultural telemetry platform, the interface balances visceral earthen roots with algorithmic rigor.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The aesthetic projects calm authority, communal transparency, and grounded optimism. Farmers, cooperative leads, and regional agronomists must feel immediate legibility under harsh outdoor daylight, uncompromised structural clarity, and zero cognitive friction during urgent climate alerts (salinity intrusion, flooding thresholds, seasonal drought).

### Design Style: Agrarian Precision & Tonal Layering
The style blends utilitarian data density with organic warmth:
- **Tonal Substrates:** A foundation of warm natural paper evokes traditional field ledgers and dry soil maps, sidestepping sterile clinical whites or harsh synthetic dark modes.
- **Architectural Framing:** Clean, pale cards resting on tinted field backgrounds, structured by subtle hairline rules that echo irrigation canals and dike systems.
- **Data Clarity:** Monospaced telemetry blocks paired with crisp humanist sans typography offer immediate computational integrity for risk curves, predictive salinity indexes, and localized Avalanche consensus proofs.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the agro-ecological lifecycle of the Mekong Delta: deep mature paddies, nutrient-rich riverbeds, vibrant new shoots, and critical environmental states.

### Primary & Functional Roles
- **Primary Rice Field Green (`#1A654D`):** Primary interactive elements, verified telemetry indicators, primary actions, and brand touchpoints.
- **Dark Forest Moss (`#0E2C24`):** High-contrast structural framing, foundational dark surfaces, persistent sidebars, and maximum-contrast typographic headers.
- **Young Rice Shoot Green (`#D5E99E`):** Dynamic high-visibility accents, focus halos, selection rings, current-phase indicators, and micro-metric badges.
- **Warm Natural Paper (`#F5F5EE`):** The foundational global canvas tone, mitigating outdoor eye fatigue and grounding all UI surfaces in an organic medium.
- **Clean White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated interactive cards, telemetry viewports, and modal layers.

### Environmental Status Colors
- **Emerald Safe (`#22C55E`):** Optimal soil nitrogen, benign water levels, baseline salinity (<0.5 g/L), and validated Avalanche consensus transactions.
- **Warning Golden Harvest (`#F59E0B`):** Moderate drought alerts, delayed irrigation windows, and intermediate river salinity risks.
- **Extreme Silt Red (`#EF4444`):** Severe saline intrusion, critical dike overflow thresholds, and urgent mitigation callouts.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme functional legibility across varied mobile devices and field terminals.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines & Interface UI:** Rendered in **Inter**, configured with tight tracking at larger scales to maintain structural density.
- **Numeric Telemetry & Smart Contract State:** Rendered in **IBM Plex Sans** (with dedicated monospace data stylings) to present coordinates, salinity readouts (e.g., `3.4 g/L`), water heights (`+1.12m`), and Avalanche transaction hashes with technical precision.
- **Field Micro-Labels:** Styled in high-contrast uppercase `label-sm` configurations to anchor card sections, sensor clusters, and geographic zones without dominating layout density.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a disciplined fluid-responsive structure engineered to handle complex data visualizations alongside rapid touch-based field inputs.

### Grid Framework
- **Mobile (up to 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch targets adhere to a minimum 48px baseline to support one-handed field operation.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Primary climate gauges split into dual-column inspection layouts.
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column layout capped at 1440px maximum viewport width with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates multi-pane geographic information systems (GIS), Avalanche state confirmation feeds, and simulation controls.

### Spacing Rhythm
Empty space functions as an active structural boundary. Card interiors employ uniform `space-lg` padding on desktop scaled to `space-md` on mobile, maintaining a clean visual rhythm that separates sensor telemetry arrays from navigational actions.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system rejects heavy, artificial drop shadows in favor of architectural surface stratification and delicate boundary definition.

### Tonal Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Warm Natural Paper (`#F5F5EE`). Raw backdrop for platform navigation and regional boundaries.
- **Layer 1 (Card / Content Plate):** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a calibrated 1px hairline border of `rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.08)`. This creates razor-sharp perimeter delineation without visual weight.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Simulation Sheets):** White (`#FFFFFF`) with a compound ambient elevation: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals / Emergency Hazard Overlays):** White (`#FFFFFF`) elevated by `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.12)`, anchored over a darkened scrim of `rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.40)`.

### Border Integrity
Every card and surface utilizes micro-borders tinted with the secondary Dark Forest Moss at low opacities (`rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.08)` to `0.15`), ensuring contrast remains crisp when devices transition between direct sunlight and indoor farm sheds.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` / `4px` default radius, `0.5rem` / `8px` for `rounded-lg`, and `0.75rem` / `12px` for `rounded-xl`).

### Architectural Discipline
- Soft, measured corners preserve geometric discipline and mirror structured plot matrices and water-gate engineering.
- Buttons, input containers, and micro-metrics inherit `rounded-md` (`6px`) or `rounded-lg` (`8px`).
- Deep pill shapes are restricted exclusively to numeric status badges and simulation toggle pills to clearly differentiate non-interactive alerts from structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Rice Field Green (`#1A654D`), text in `#FFFFFF`, corner radius `rounded-lg` (8px). Hover state transitions to Dark Forest Moss (`#0E2C24`). Active state uses `scale(0.99)`.
- **Accent / Simulation Action:** Background in Young Rice Shoot Green (`#D5E99E`), text in Dark Forest Moss (`#0E2C24`), font weight `600`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1px border in `rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.20)`, text in `#1A654D`. Hover introduces a subtle tint fill: `rgba(26, 101, 77, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Data Containers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` base plates with an explicit 1px outer rule of `rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.08)` and `rounded-lg` geometry.
- Section headers within cards feature a bottom divider of `1px solid rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.05)` with `space-md` inner padding.
- Interactive cards elevate subtly to Layer 2 on hover, accompanied by a border shift to `rgba(26, 101, 77, 0.3)`.

### Chips & Telemetry Status Badges
- **Safe:** Surface `#DCFCE7` (`#22C55E` at 15%), text `#15803D`, 1px border in `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.25)`.
- **Warning:** Surface `#FEF3C7` (`#F59E0B` at 15%), text `#B45309`, 1px border in `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.30)`.
- **Critical / Saline Incursion:** Surface `#FEE2E2` (`#EF4444` at 15%), text `#B91C1C`, 1px border in `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.30)`.
- Typography adheres to `label-sm` or `data-mono-sm` with tabular lining figures.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Sensor Sliders:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border in `rgba(14, 44, 36, 0.16)`, height 44px (field accessible). Focused states display an immediate 2px outer ring in `#1A654D` with an offset of 1px.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px hit zones, checked background `#1A654D` containing high-contrast white ticks. Radio controls feature a centered `#D5E99E` indicator dot when engaged.

### Specialized Agricultural Components
- **Salinity Gauge Card:** Prominent display of current parts-per-thousand (ppt / g/L) utilizing `data-mono-lg`, integrated alongside an Avalanche consensus verification marker (`Tx Verified`) rendered in `label-sm`.
- **Climate Risk Trajectory Sparklines:** Minimal hairline charts utilizing `#1A654D` for historic data and `#F59E0B`/`#EF4444` for forward predictive risk bands over a 72-hour forecast window.
